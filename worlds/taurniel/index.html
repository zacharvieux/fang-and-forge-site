TAURNIEL

Every great story begins somewhere.

Begin where familiar faces gather, the roads are well worn, and the world beyond the last lantern remains wonderfully uncertain. Taurniel is an emerging tabletop setting built for discovery, shared stories, and journeys that grow far beyond their beginnings.

A world to grow into

Start somewhere familiar. Go somewhere extraordinary.

Taurniel is being shaped around one of tabletop fantasy's enduring ideas: a place where adventurers can learn the names of their neighbors before learning the names of distant kingdoms.

Picture a small, lively settlement at the meeting of travel, trade, and untamed countryside. Markets open at dawn. Stories pass between travelers. Beyond town, quiet paths and unfamiliar horizons invite the curious to take their first steps.

Creative preview: This introduction is sample material, not established Taurniel canon. The worldbuilder will determine the setting's official places, people, history, and rules.

Possibilities at the table

A few whispers for the road.

Original, optional adventure prompts to suggest the tone. None are fixed world lore.

Rumor I

A parcel arrives addressed to someone nobody in town remembers—along with directions to a road missing from every current map.

Rumor II

On moonless evenings, a distant lantern appears in the hills. Each night, it shines from somewhere closer.

Rumor III

A traveling merchant returns with one extra wagon in the caravan. None of the drivers will say where it came from.

Rumor IV

After heavy rain, old steps emerge beneath a fallen tree, descending into stone that is strangely dry.
