THE EASTERN EXPANSE

Wonders beyond the mountains.

East across the Fulmannon Mountains lie several harsh and unforgiving lands filled with exotic magics and monstrous beasts.

From the ancient temples of the Baas Forest through the sands of the Lushâra to the strange and marvelous Fourfold, discoveries wait for those willing to seek them: long-lost monkish sects, desert tombs, reclusive cultures, and crystalline structures unlike anything found elsewhere.

LANDS OF THE EXPANSE

Three extraordinary frontiers.

Fire, jungle, and celestial crystal shape three very different lands east of the Fulmannon Mountains.

THE LUSHAYR

Lushâra Desert

A vast and unforgiving expanse of golden dunes and sun-baked plains, fierce by day and cold beneath an immense canopy of stars at night.

The Grand Sultanate of Lakari controls lush eastern cities and coastal oases, while the Dolqadír slavers prey upon caravans crossing the deep western desert near the Fulmannon Mountains.

LAKARI · DOLQADÍR · OASES & CARAVANS
THE ANCIENT GREEN

Baas Forest

An immense wilderness beneath a canopy so dense that daylight reaches the ground only in scattered shafts of gold. Warm rain feeds blackwater streams, misty pools, tangled rivers, and fern-choked valleys flowing toward Lake Ushon.

Deep within forest-cloaked mountains, stories persist of secluded monks preserving ancient ways. The people of the surrounding lands say the forest is older than their oldest kingdoms—and that only Ushon remembers.

LAKE USHON · ANCIENT TEMPLES · HIDDEN SECTS
THE CRYSTALLINE VALLEY

The Fourfold

A lush valley shaped around a sheer-sided canyon containing Aelith, an alien crystalline plinth that once tore violently through the earth. Crystal-veined fragments scattered across the land still influence water, flame, stone, wind, and growing things.

The Aelithi, whose origin was infused by the meteor's magic, build settlements across the valley. Their largest settlement surrounds Aelith itself, where a beautiful crystal palace is carved from the plinth.

AELITH · AELITHI · ELEMENTAL CRYSTAL
