The world today

Peace has returned. The world has changed.

It has been 412 years since the Great Rift tore through the Planes, and 20 years since the Dragon Wars came to an end. Across the Fertile Coast and beyond, communities are thriving again as goods and services flow with minimal regulation.

With armies no longer constantly marching through the countryside, the wilderness has begun to reclaim its own. Trade brings opportunity, but bandits threaten rural roads. Travelers may also encounter goblins, owlbears, orcs, giants, and werebeasts.

The Age of Rebirth is a time of prosperity and possibility, but the safety of a city does not extend to every road beyond its walls.

Fulmannon · Western coast

The Fertile Coast

A fruitful coastal region on the western edge of the continent of Fulmannon, home to distinct nations and peoples: from the cold island of Brähm and the red lands of the Adamant Realms to the lowlands of Taurniel and lands farther south.

The descriptions below reflect the available creator-supplied country lore. Unfinished entries have not been filled with invented material.

01 / KINGDOMFERTILE COAST

Taurniel

A rugged, prosperous kingdom of green hills, misty moors, dense forests, and fertile lowlands. Feudal baronies owe allegiance to King Laurenal, who rules from the capital of Tiergard.

Walled townships, manors, and remote villages dot the countryside. The city of Thuburabe rises around an imposing ziggurat containing temples devoted to every known god in Thulohm.

Capital Tiergard Noted city Thuburabe Peoples Humans, Halflings, Elves, Dwarves
02 / CONFEDERATIONFERTILE COAST

Adamant Realms

A vast, sun-scorched region of red mesas, dusty steppes, and barren volcanic ranges. Rather than a single kingdom, the land is home to a confederation of ancient Dwarven kingdoms, nomadic Dragonborn tribes, and a few Dark Elf settlements.

Dwarven miners, masons, smiths, and traders send metals, gemstones, weapons, and stonework to neighboring lands. Dragonborn herders raise hardy Fulgoats. The reasons behind the Dark Elves' presence on the surface remain uncertain.

Terrain Red mesas, steppes, volcanic mountains Known for Smithing, trade, Fulgoat herding
03 / ISLAND NATIONAVAREL SEA

Brähm

A small, green island nation amid the cold waters of the Avarel Sea. Meadows, sheltered coves, rocky shores, and fishing villages shelter communities of Humans, Turttlekin, and Otterfolk.

Steep-roofed timber and stone homes withstand strong sea winds. Small harbors, fishing boats, smokehouses, and communal festivals shape daily life. Brähm is known for hospitality and seamanship and takes little interest in the affairs of greater nations.

Peoples Humans, Turttlekin, Otterfolk Known for Fishing, coastal life, community festivals

People & homeland

The peoples of Thulohm

Families and communities have spread across these lands for millennia. The campaign introduction offers several possible origins for the adventurers beginning their journey to Tiergard.

These are the backgrounds mentioned in the supplied campaign notes, not an exhaustive list of Thulohm's inhabitants or character options.

Humans & Halflings

Among the most widespread peoples in Thulohm, with ancestors established across the world for millennia.

High Elves

Some descend from immigrants long settled in Taurniel; others travel from the mighty kingdoms far to the south.

Wood Elves

Found in the towns and cities of Taurniel and among tribes in the Kryger wildlands to the south.

Dwarves

Have established communities throughout Taurniel's cities, as well as ancestral kingdoms in the Adamant Realms.

Dragonborn

Many hail from tribes near the site of the Great Rift in the Adamant Realms. Some families have settled in Taurniel's larger cities. Their connection to the Dragon Wars continues to shape how they are treated: acceptance has improved, but prejudice remains.

A campaign set within Thulohm

The Journey Begins

Aboard the Fairchild

Four first-level adventurers, barely acquainted, meet aboard the Fairchild as they travel by ship to Tiergard, capital of the kingdom of Taurniel. Each is making the journey for a reason of their own.

From this starting point, the world opens to the choices of the group. This is a campaign introduction, not the complete story of Thulohm.

Campaign approach

Playing in Thulohm

The supplied campaign is a classic sandbox in a massive, expanding world. The players choose where to go and how to pursue their objectives; leaving an expected path is part of the experience.

These labels describe the creator's intended emphasis for this campaign—not mechanical rules, world-wide restrictions, or ratings of quality.

01 / WORLD POWERSHIGH

Politics

Factions, kingdoms, and other powers have their own competing interests. Politics influence the background of the campaign without being its sole focus.

02 / COMBAT PRESENTATIONLOW

Tactics

Combat is expected, but it is currently presented mostly through theater of the mind rather than miniatures. More tactical play may be possible in the future.

03 / THE JOURNEYHIGH

Overland Survival

Travel spans wilderness, unfamiliar encounters, and developed cities. Date and time are tracked, and Thulohm has its own lunar cycle.

04 / INTERACTIONMEDIUM

Roleplay

Social encounters and negotiation matter. How a character responds to others can affect outcomes as much as a die roll; acting is not required.

05 / YOUR CHOICESHIGH

Self-Direction

The world contains many powers and many goals. Players determine which opportunities to pursue, and their decisions can affect events and even policies in the kingdoms around them.
