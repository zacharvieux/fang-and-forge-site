WELCOME TO THULOHM

A world returning to life.

Four centuries after the Great Rift tore through the Planes, Thulohm stands in an age of recovery and abundance.

Twenty years have passed since the Dragon Wars. Across the Fertile Coast, communities thrive again and trade moves between nations with few restrictions. Cities and kingdoms are rebuilding the lives that war disrupted.

But peace has brought its own changes. With armies no longer marching through the countryside, the wilds are growing wild again. Bandits prey upon rural roads, and travelers face dangers beyond the safety of the towns.

THE WESTERN EDGE OF FULMANNON

Discover the Fertile Coast.

Across the western edge of Fulmannon, fertile lowlands give way to red volcanic kingdoms, island harbors, ancient forests, and the sea-cliff settlements of the Elven Triumvirate. Each land tells a different story.

THE GREEN KINGDOM

Taurniel

Green hills, misty moors, dense forests, and fertile lowlands stretch across feudal baronies loyal to King Laurenal in Tiergard. Walled townships, sprawling manors, and church-centered villages dot the countryside.

Its jewel is Thuburabe, a great city at the base of an imposing ziggurat holding temples to every known god in Thulohm. Humans, Halflings, Elves, and Dwarves form much of this proud kingdom's population.

KINGDOM · CAPITAL: TIERGARD
THE RED LANDS

Adamant Realms

Across sun-scorched red mesas, dusty steppes, and volcanic mountains lies a confederation of ancient Dwarven kingdoms, nomadic Dragonborn tribes, and a few Dark Elf settlements.

Dwarven caravans carry metals, gemstones, weapons, and stonework to neighboring lands, while Dragonborn herders tend their hardy Fulgoats. Few know what has brought the Dark Elves to the surface.

DWARVEN KINGDOMS · DRAGONBORN TRIBES
THE ISLAND NATION

Brähm

Amid the cold waters of the Avarel Sea, meadows, coves, and rocky shores shelter close-knit communities of Humans, Turttlekin, and Otterfolk, whose steep-roofed timber and stone homes weather the persistent sea winds.

Fishing boats fill its little harbors, and smokehouses carry the scent of herring, salmon, and salted cod. Brähm is celebrated for hospitality, seamanship, and festivals that follow the rhythm of the tides.

THE ANCIENT WILDLANDS

Kryger

Heavily unmapped forests and marshlands form a great tangle of gnarled branches, thickets, and brambles. An ancient magic lives here, known to the mysteriously reclusive druids who dwell within its woods.

The Wood Elves are accomplished hunters and trappers, living in harmony with the forest's flora and fauna. Among the Kryger's most sacred treasures is the Krygew tree, an ancient, gnarled hardwood of which only a few specimens are known to exist.

THE ELVEN COAST

Elven Triumvirate

Towering evergreen forests, mist-shrouded valleys, and sheer mountains meet the cold, restless Avarel Sea. Elven cities and villages nestle among trees and cliffs, linked by winding paths, stone bridges, and waterways woven into the landscape.

Coastal havens shelter slender ships, while secluded river valleys cradle gardens, waterfalls, and ancient woodland. The Triumvirate is a land of preserved groves and quiet harmony, its white-sailed vessels crossing distant seas.

A WORLD OF MANY PEOPLES

Every journey has a homeland.

Humans and Halflings have lived across Thulohm for millennia. High Elves have connections to the southern kingdoms, while Wood Elves are found throughout Taurniel and the Kryger wildlands. Dwarves are established in Taurniel's cities and the kingdoms of the Adamant Realms.

The Dragon Wars remain a living memory for the Dragonborn. Many trace their origins to tribes near the Great Rift in the Adamant mountains; others have settled in the larger towns and cities of Taurniel, where acceptance has grown but prejudice has not wholly disappeared.

A JOURNEY INTO THULOHM

Four strangers.
One ship.
Every road ahead.

The Fairchild sails toward Tiergard, capital of Taurniel. Aboard are four first-level adventurers who have only just met, each traveling for reasons of their own.

Beyond their arrival lies a world of kingdoms and competing powers, distant cities, rugged wilderness, and encounters that will test more than steel. Where they go—and how they change the world around them—is theirs to decide.
