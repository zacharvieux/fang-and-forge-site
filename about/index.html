About Fang & Forge

We are building a home for our worlds and the tools used to explore them.

Fang & Forge Productions is an independent creative studio focused on worldbuilding, storytelling, tabletop gaming, and digital tools.

What we're doing

Our goal is to develop original worlds and projects under one studio while allowing each world to grow into its own identity. The site will evolve alongside those projects rather than being limited to a single campaign or game.

Where we're starting

Veyra is the first world being brought into the Fang & Forge platform. At the same time, we are developing tabletop and virtual tabletop projects that can support our games and future creative work.
