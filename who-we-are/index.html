Who we are

Worlds are where we started. Fang & Forge is where they meet.

Fang & Forge Productions is an independent creative studio building original worlds, stories, tabletop experiences, and digital tools.

Our story

Built from the things we wanted to create ourselves.

Fang & Forge grew out of worldbuilding, tabletop campaigns, writing, and the constant process of creating tools to support those ideas. What began as individual projects is becoming a shared studio where those worlds and tools can live together, grow over time, and eventually become finished releases.

We are building this deliberately and in public. Some projects begin as campaign material, some as experiments, and some as ideas that deserve a larger home. Fang & Forge is the place where we can take those ideas seriously and develop them into something other people can explore, play, read, and use.

What we create

More than one kind of project.

Original WorldsSettings, lore, locations, characters, cultures, maps, and histories built to support stories and play.
Tabletop ExperiencesCampaign material, rules, adventures, tools, and resources designed around the way people actually play.
Stories & BooksFiction and longer-form projects that let our worlds exist beyond the table.
Digital ToolsFoundry VTT work, web tools, and eventually original software that supports players, GMs, and creators.
