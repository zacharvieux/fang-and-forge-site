Who we are

Making room for every idea worth creating.

Fang & Forge Productions is an independent studio for original worlds, stories, tabletop games, digital experiences, and creative productions that can grow into something more.

Our story

Built from the things we wanted to create ourselves.

Fang & Forge grew out of worldbuilding, tabletop campaigns, writing, and the constant urge to turn ideas into something people can experience. Rather than organizing the entire studio around a single setting or game, we're creating a home for different projects, collaborators, and creative formats.

We are building this deliberately and in public. Some projects begin as campaign material, some as experiments, and some as ideas that deserve a larger home. Fang & Forge is the place where we can take those ideas seriously and develop them into something other people can explore, play, read, and use.

What we create

More than one kind of project.

Original WorldsSettings, lore, locations, characters, cultures, maps, and histories built to support stories and play.
Tabletop ExperiencesCampaign material, rules, adventures, tools, and resources designed around the way people actually play.
