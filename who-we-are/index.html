Who we are

Making room for every idea worth creating.

Fang & Forge Productions is an independent studio for original worlds, stories, tabletop games, digital experiences, and creative productions that can grow into something more.

Our story

Built from the things we wanted to create ourselves.

Fang & Forge grew out of worldbuilding, tabletop campaigns, writing, and the constant urge to turn ideas into something people can experience. Rather than organizing the entire studio around a single setting or game, we're creating a home for different projects, collaborators, and creative formats.

We are building this deliberately and in public. Some projects begin as campaign material, some as experiments, and some as ideas that deserve a larger home. Fang & Forge is the place where we can take those ideas seriously and develop them into something other people can explore, play, read, and use.

Meet the founders

The people behind the forge.

Fang & Forge is currently led by two founders bringing different strengths to the same goal: building worlds, stories, games, and tools people want to spend time with.

Co-Founder

Zachariah “Zach” Arvieux

Zach focuses on turning creative ideas into working experiences. His work at Fang & Forge includes the studio website, digital tools, virtual tabletop projects, production systems, and helping shape how the studio’s worlds are presented and shared.

He brings a technical and production-minded approach to the studio, working at the point where worldbuilding, design, and technology meet.

FocusWeb · Digital tools · VTT · Production
Co-Founder

Taylor

Taylor focuses on worldbuilding, lore, and creative development. His work helps define the places, cultures, histories, and adventure ideas that give Fang & Forge projects their identity.

He brings the setting-first creative perspective that turns broad concepts into worlds with enough depth to support stories, campaigns, and future projects.

FocusWorldbuilding · Lore · Creative development
